When you buy the AI Adoption Workshop Kit, you receive a license to use its materials based on the tier you choose. All tiers include future updates to the kit during its early-access version.

Individual Facilitator License

For one person running their own workshops, training sessions, or internal team conversations.

  • Use the full kit (facilitator guide, slide deck, participant materials, prompt pack, checklists, and worksheets) in your own sessions.
  • Personal, single-facilitator use.
  • Not for resale or use as a paid deliverable for external clients.

Professional Consultant License

For consultants, trainers, and facilitators using the kit with paying clients.

  • Everything in the Individual license.
  • Use with external client workshops, and permission to adapt delivery for client contexts.
  • Permission to use participant-facing materials during client sessions.
  • You may not resell or redistribute the source materials themselves as a standalone product.

Organization License

For one organization using the kit internally across multiple teams or departments.

  • Everything in the Consultant license, for internal use.
  • Use by multiple internal facilitators and reuse across internal sessions.
  • Internal adaptation for your company context.
  • Covers one organization; it does not grant rights to deliver the kit as a paid service to third parties.

Updates

Early buyers receive future updates to the kit at no extra cost during the early-access period.
