The adoption gap

Everyone has tried AI. Almost no one has changed how they work.

Pilots, demos, and enthusiasm fade because nothing connects the tools to the actual work. The result is uneven usage, quiet skepticism, and no measurable change.

01

Tool overload, no direction

Teams collect subscriptions but lack a shared sense of what to actually use them for.

02

Excitement without habits

A spark of interest rarely survives the return to everyday deadlines and old routines.

03

No way to measure progress

Without a baseline, leaders can't tell whether adoption is improving or stalling.

How the workshop works

A clear arc from curiosity to commitment

01

Map the real work

Participants list the recurring tasks they actually own — the starting point for everything that follows.

15 min
02

Match tasks to tools

Using the kit's reference cards, teams pair each task with a sensible AI approach — and note where it doesn't belong.

25 min
03

Design the workflow

Each small group drafts one concrete workflow they'll use next week, including hand-offs and quality checks.

30 min
04

Commit & measure

Teams set a baseline on the scorecard and agree on a 30-day follow-up so the habit takes hold.

20 min
